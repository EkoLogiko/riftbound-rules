![[Costs#^cr-204-3-a]]

Example: Bullet Time is a [[Spell|spell]] that reads “Pay any amount of &#91;A&#93; to [[Deal|deal]] that much [[Damage|damage]] to all [[Enemy|enemy]] [[Unit|units]] at a [[Battlefield|battlefield]].” As the spell resolves, its controller pays any amount of &#91;A&#93; as a [[Costs|cost]], and deals that much damage to all units at a battlefield as the effect.

Example: Ancient Henge is a [[Gear|gear]] that reads “&#91;[[Reaction (Keyword)|Reaction]]&#93;&#91;>&#93; &#91;E&#93;: Pay any amount of [[Energy]] to Add that much &#91;A&#93;.” As the [[Ability|ability]] resolves, its controller pays any amount of Energy as a [[Costs|cost]], and Adds that much &#91;A&#93; as the effect.

Example: Insightful Investigator is a [[Unit|unit]] that reads “When you [[Play|play]] me, choose an opponent. They [[Reveal|reveal]] their [[Hand|hand]]. You may pay 2 XP to choose a [[Card|card]] from their hand. If you do, they [[Discard|discard]] that card and [[Draw|draw]] 1.” As the [[Ability|ability]] resolves, its controller may pay 2 XP as a [[Costs|cost]], and chooses a card from that player’s hand as the effect.
