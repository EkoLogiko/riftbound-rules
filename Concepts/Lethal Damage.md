Example: A [[Unit|unit]] has 5 &#91;M&#93; and 3 [[Damage|damage]] marked on it. Frigid Touch is played [[Targeting|targeting]] that unit. When it resolves, the unit’s [[Might]] becomes 3, and it will have lethal damage marked on it.

Example: A [[Unit|unit]] has 0 &#91;M&#93;. In order to have lethal damage marked on it, it must have at least 1 [[Damage|damage]] marked on it.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 142.4.b · PDF page 18" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 142.4.b · PDF page 18</div></details>

![[Unit#^cr-142-4-c]]

Example: Elder Dragon’s [[Passive Ability|passive ability]] reads “Any amount of your [[Damage|damage]] is enough to [[Kill|kill]] [[Enemy|enemy]] [[Unit|units]].” This alters the Lethal Damage value for enemy units that have damage marked by you.
