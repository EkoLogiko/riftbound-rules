![[Deck Construction#^cr-103-3-a-2]]

Example: Loose Cannon has the [[Tag|tag]] Jinx. Therefore, a player could choose Jinx, Rebel or Jinx, Demolitionist as their Chosen Champion, because they also have the tag Jinx.

Example: Tibbers has the [[Tag|tag]] Annie, but it is a [[Signature|signature]] [[Unit|unit]], not a [[Champion|champion unit]]. It cannot be your Chosen Champion, even if your [[Champion Legend]] has the tag Annie.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 103.3.a.2 · PDF page 2-3" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 103.3.a.2 · PDF page 2-3</div></details>

![[Deck Construction#^cr-103-3-a-3]]

Example: A player chose Jinx, Rebel as their Chosen Champion. One [[Copy|copy]] of Jinx, Rebel starts the game in their [[Champion Zone|Chosen Champion zone]]. Any additional copies of Jinx, Rebel in their [[Main Deck]], their [[Hand|hand]], their [[Trash|trash]], or on [[Board|the board]] also count as their Chosen Champion for any game rules and effects that care about Chosen Champion status during the game.
