Example: A player plays Alpha Strike, which reads in part “Choose a [[Friendly|friendly]] [[Unit|unit]]. It deals [[Damage|damage]] equal to its [[Might]] split among [[Enemy|enemy]] units at [[Battlefield|battlefields]].” They target their 5 &#91;M&#93; unit and five 1 &#91;M&#93; enemy Recruits at battlefields. In reaction, their opponent plays Feral Strength [[Targeting|targeting]] one of their recruits, and Frigid Touch targeting the 5 &#91;M&#93; unit. When Alpha Strike resolves, they have 3 damage to split, and must choose which targets cease being targets. They can’t choose for 4 of the units to cease being targets so that they can [[Deal|deal]] 3 to the 3 &#91;M&#93; recruit. They can only choose at most 2 of the targets to cease being targets.
