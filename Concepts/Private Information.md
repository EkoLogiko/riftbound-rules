# Private Information

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]]

## From Privacy

![[Privacy#^cr-128-4]]

Example: [[Card|Cards]] in a player's [[Hand|hand]] are owned by that player. Only that player may read or look at their faces.

Example: If a player controls a facedown [[Card|card]] at a [[Battlefield|battlefield]], that player and only that player may read or look at that card's face, regardless of who owns that card.
