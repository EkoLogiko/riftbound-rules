# Optional Additional Costs

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]]

## From Process of Playing a Card

![[Process of Playing a Card#^cr-356-2-b-1]]

Example: A [[Unit|unit]] has the [[Ability|ability]] "As you [[Play|play]] me, you may [[Discard|discard]] 1 as an [[Additional Cost|additional cost]]. If you do, reduce my [[Costs|cost]] by &#91;2&#93;." While playing the unit, its controller declares their intention to pay the additional cost in step 2, applies that additional cost in [[Process of Playing a Card#^cr-356-2|Process of Playing a Card]], applies the discount granted by paying that cost in [[Process of Playing a Card#^cr-356-4|Process of Playing a Card]], and discards a [[Card|card]] to pay that additional cost in [[Process of Playing a Card#^cr-357-2|Process of Playing a Card]].

See [[Passive Ability#^cr-363|Passive Abilities]] for more information.
