![[Process of Playing a Card#^cr-359-3-f-2]]

Examples: A player moves Yasuo, Remorseful to an occupied [[Enemy|enemy]] [[Battlefield|battlefield]] and initiates [[Combat|combat]] there. In reaction to the Yasuo, Remorseful [[Attack Trigger|attack trigger]], their opponent plays Fight or Flight from facedown [[Targeting|targeting]] Yasuo, [[Movement|moving]] him back to [[Base|base]]. When the attack trigger resolves, “here” is no longer the battlefield where combat is ongoing and the attack trigger mistargets. In reaction to a Yasuo, Remorseful attack trigger, an opponent plays Stupefy targeting Yasuo. When Yasuo’s attack trigger resolves, it will [[Deal|deal]] [[Damage|damage]] equal to his current [[Might]] of 5.

Example: Lillia, Fae Fawn reads “when I [[Move|move]] from a [[Location|location]], [[Play|play]] a 3 &#91;M&#93; Sprite [[Token|token]] with [[Temporary]] there.” If Lillia moves to a [[Battlefield|battlefield]], her [[Triggered Ability|triggered ability]] will be placed on the [[Chain|chain]] and it will note the location she moved from when it does so. If she moves to a non-board [[Zone|zone]] in reaction to the triggered ability on the chain, it will not affect where the Sprite token will be played when the triggered ability resolves.

Example: Iascylla reads “When I [[Hold|hold]], at the start of your next [[Main Phase]], you may [[Move|move]] an [[Enemy|enemy]] [[Unit|unit]] to this [[Battlefield|battlefield]].” The “this battlefield” in her delayed [[Triggered Ability|triggered ability]] refers to the battlefield she held, and so will be referenced from the trigger condition, when the triggered ability is generated.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 359.3.f.3.b · PDF page 61" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 359.3.f.3.b · PDF page 61</div></details>

![[Process of Playing a Card#^cr-359-3-f-4]]

Example: Yasuo, Remorseful reads “when I attack, [[Deal|deal]] [[Damage|damage]] equal to my [[Might]] to an [[Enemy|enemy]] [[Unit|unit]] here.” Yasuo moves to an occupied enemy [[Battlefield|battlefield]] and his [[Attack Trigger|attack trigger]] goes on the [[Chain|chain]]. In reaction to the attack trigger, the [[Defender|defending]] player plays a [[Hidden|hidden]] Hostile Takeover and gains [[Control|control]] of Yasuo. The [[Triggered Ability|triggered ability]] is unaffected by Yasuo changing controllers, and “enemy” is in reference to the triggered ability itself, so it will resolve with no issue.

Example: In reaction to the same Yasuo, Remorseful trigger, say the [[Defender|defending]] player had instead played a [[Spell|spell]] that reads “&#91;[[Reaction (Keyword)|Reaction]]&#93;. Gain [[Control|control]] of a [[Triggered Ability|triggered ability]]. You may make new choices for it.” They chose the [[Attack Trigger|attack trigger]]. When Yasuo’s attack trigger resolves, if they didn’t make new choices for the trigger, the controller of the triggered ability will no longer be an [[Enemy|enemy]] to the targeted [[Unit|unit]], so the triggered ability will mistarget and do nothing. If they instead chose Yasuo with the attack trigger, he would be an enemy unit to the triggered ability and so it would [[Deal|deal]] [[Damage|damage]] equal to his [[Might]] to himself.
