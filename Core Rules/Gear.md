Gear have a [[Location]]. ^cr-152

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 152 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 152 · PDF page 22</div></details>

A Gear’s [[Location]] is the [[Base]] or [[Battlefield]] it currently occupies. ^cr-152-1

See [[Location#^cr-197|Locations]] for more information.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 152.1 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 152.1 · PDF page 22</div></details>

Non-Unit Gear cannot normally become located at a [[Battlefield]] unless by some special means. ^cr-152-2

**Example:** Gear are played to [[Base]] unless an effect specifies otherwise. A gear played from Facedown, which is specified to be played to the [[Battlefield]] it was played from, will enter at that Battlefield. Gear can also become located at a Battlefield if they are [[Attached|attached]] to a [[Unit|unit]] who becomes located at a Battlefield. A Gear that is a Unit can [[Move|move]] to a Battlefield.

See [[Hidden#^cr-811|Hidden]] for more information.

See [[Attachment#^cr-716|Attachment]] for more information.
