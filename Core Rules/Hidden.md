# Hidden

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Core Rules Index|Core rules]]

<span title="Core rule 811"></span> ^cr-811

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811 · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811 · PDF page 129</div></details>

Hidden is a [[Keywords|keyword]] that acts as a prerequisite to perform the [[Hide]] [[Discretionary Action]]. ^cr-811-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1 · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1 · PDF page 129</div></details>

It is present on [[Spell|Spells]], [[Unit|Units]], and [[Gear]]. ^cr-811-1-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.a · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.a · PDF page 129</div></details>

It is functionally short for "While this [[Card|card]] is in your [[Hand|hand]] or in your [[Champion Zone]] on your [[Turn|turn]] during an [[Open State]], you may pay &#91;A&#93; to [[Hide|hide]] this facedown at a [[Battlefield|battlefield]] you [[Control|control]] that doesn't already have a facedown card hidden there for as long as you control that battlefield. Beginning on the next turn, this gains &#91;[[Reaction (Keyword)|Reaction]]&#93; and you may [[Play|play]] this, ignoring its [[Base Cost|base cost]]." ^cr-811-1-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.b · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.b · PDF page 129</div></details>

It allows the player to take the [[Discretionary Action]] [[Hide]]. ^cr-811-1-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.c · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.c · PDF page 129</div></details>

[[Hide]] is not a subset of [[Play]]. ^cr-811-1-c-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.c.1 · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.c.1 · PDF page 129</div></details>

Hiding a [[Card|card]] does not open a [[Chain|chain]]. ^cr-811-1-c-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.c.2 · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.c.2 · PDF page 129</div></details>

Playing a [[Card|card]] from facedown (or "from Hidden") does open a [[Chain|chain]]. ^cr-811-1-c-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.c.3 · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.c.3 · PDF page 129</div></details>

Some choices made while playing a [[Card|card]] from facedown are restricted to the [[Battlefield|battlefield]] where it was hidden. A card cannot be played from facedown if it is a [[Spell|spell]] with no valid targets under these restrictions. ^cr-811-1-d

See [[Process of Playing a Card#^cr-355-6|Targeting]] for more information.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.d · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.d · PDF page 129</div></details>

A hidden [[Permanent|permanent]] must be played to that [[Battlefield|battlefield]]. ^cr-811-1-d-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.d.1 · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.d.1 · PDF page 129</div></details>

This includes hidden [[Gear|gear]], and overrides the normal restriction that gear have in only being allowed to be played to [[Base|base]]. ^cr-811-1-d-1-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.d.1.a · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.d.1.a · PDF page 129</div></details>

If a hidden [[Spell|spell]] chooses any targets, those targets must be chosen from among options at the [[Battlefield|battlefield]] it was played from, unless it explicitly restricts [[Targeting|targeting]] in such a way that makes this impossible. ^cr-811-1-d-2

**Example:** Emperor’s Divide is a [[Spell|spell]] with hidden that reads in part “[[Move]] any number of [[Friendly|friendly]] [[Unit|units]] at a [[Battlefield|battlefield]] to their [[Base|base]].” When this spell is played from facedown, all units chosen for the spell must be located at the same battlefield it was played from.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.d.2 · PDF page 129" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.d.2 · PDF page 129</div></details>

If a [[Play Effect|play effect]] of a hidden [[Permanent|permanent]] chooses any targets, those targets must be chosen from among options at the [[Battlefield|battlefield]] it was played to, unless the [[Ability|ability]] explicitly restricts [[Targeting|targeting]] in a way that makes this impossible. ^cr-811-1-d-3

**Example:** Blastcone Fae is a [[Unit|unit]] with Hidden and “When you [[Play|play]] me, give a unit -2 &#91;M&#93; this [[Turn|turn]], to a minimum of 1 &#91;M&#93;.” Because this is a [[Play Effect|play effect]], its target must be chosen from among units at the [[Battlefield|battlefield]] it was played to if Blastcone Fae was played from facedown.

**Example:** Tideturner is a [[Unit|unit]] with Hidden and “When you [[Play|play]] me, you may choose a unit you [[Control|control]] at another [[Location|location]]. [[Move]] me to its location and it to my original location.” Because its [[Play Effect|play effect]] has a [[Targeting|targeting]] restriction that can never be fulfilled by a unit at its [[Battlefield|battlefield]], its target may be chosen freely from among the available options.

**Example:** A [[Spell|spell]] reads in part “The next [[Unit|unit]] you [[Play|play]] this [[Turn|turn]] can be played to any [[Battlefield|battlefield]].” After playing that spell, a player plays Blastcone Fae from facedown to a battlefield other than the one where it was hidden. Blastcone Fae’s [[Play Effect|play effect]] must choose targets from among units at the battlefield Blastcone Fae was played to, not the one it was played from.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.d.3 · PDF page 130" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.d.3 · PDF page 130</div></details>

A [[Play Effect|play effect]] of a [[Permanent|permanent]] played from facedown references information from its source when determining what [[Battlefield|battlefield]] it was played from and what targets are legal. ^cr-811-1-d-3-a

**Example:** A [[Unit|unit]] reads in part “When you [[Play|play]] me, [[Kill|kill]] another [[Friendly|friendly]] unit.” If it is played from the [[Facedown Zone|facedown zone]], the [[Play Effect|play effect]] must choose a friendly unit at the [[Battlefield|battlefield]] the unit was played from. If the unit is no longer on [[Board|the board]] when the play effect attempts to finalize, it will not be able to choose a legal target. If the unit is no longer on the board when the play effect attempts to resolve, the play effect will not be able to reference the information of where the unit was played from, and it will mistarget.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.d.3.a · PDF page 130" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.d.3.a · PDF page 130</div></details>

Each target is treated separately and individually when processing this rule. ^cr-811-1-d-4

**Example:** Smoke and Mirrors is a [[Spell|spell]] that reads in part “Choose a [[Unit|unit]] you [[Control|control]] and another unit you control at a different [[Location|location]].” If Smoke and Mirrors is played from facedown, the first unit chosen can be chosen at the [[Battlefield|battlefield]] Smoke and Mirrors was played from, so it must be. The second unit chosen explicitly restricts [[Targeting|targeting]] in a way that makes this impossible, so it can be chosen from any location.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.d.4 · PDF page 130" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.d.4 · PDF page 130</div></details>

If a hidden [[Spell|spell]] or a [[Play Effect|play effect]] of a hidden [[Permanent|permanent]] causes you to [[Play|play]] a [[Unit|unit]], you must choose to play that unit at that [[Battlefield|battlefield]]. ^cr-811-1-d-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.1.d.5 · PDF page 130" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.1.d.5 · PDF page 130</div></details>

[[Ability|Abilities]] and [[Instructions|instructions]] of hidden [[Card|cards]] other than the choices listed above function as normal. ^cr-811-2

**Example:** Stand United is a [[Spell|spell]] that has Hidden and says “[[Buffs|Buff]] a [[Friendly|friendly]] [[Unit|unit]]. Buffs give an additional +1 [[Might|might]] to friendly units this [[Turn|turn]].” If it’s played from facedown, the first part of its [[Ability|ability]] must choose a friendly unit at the same [[Battlefield|battlefield]], but the second part of its ability affects all friendly units with buffs, no matter where they are.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.2 · PDF page 130" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.2 · PDF page 130</div></details>

Instead of being hidden, a [[Card|card]] with Hidden may be played for its [[Costs|cost]] as normal, at its normal timing with no restrictions on [[Targeting|targeting]]. ^cr-811-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.3 · PDF page 130" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.3 · PDF page 130</div></details>

Multiple instances of Hidden are redundant. ^cr-811-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.4 · PDF page 130" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.4 · PDF page 130</div></details>

Hidden, and whether or not a [[Card|card]] has Hidden, is a characteristic of the card and may be checked or referenced by other [[Game Effect|Game Effects]]. ^cr-811-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.5 · PDF page 130" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.5 · PDF page 130</div></details>

This is true as long as the [[Card|card]] is not facedown. A facedown card is not a card with Hidden. ^cr-811-5-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.5.a · PDF page 130" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.5.a · PDF page 130</div></details>

A [[Card|card]] that is Hidden gains Reaction [[Turn|the turn]] after it is hidden while facedown or played from facedown, and may be played any time a card with Reaction may be played as a result. ^cr-811-6

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.6 · PDF page 131" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.6 · PDF page 131</div></details>

The property is granted to the [[Card|card]] in its facedown state, and is not publicly known. ^cr-811-6-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 811.6.a · PDF page 131" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 811.6.a · PDF page 131</div></details>
