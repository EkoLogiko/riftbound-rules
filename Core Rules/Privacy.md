# Privacy

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Core Rules Index|Core rules]]

<span title="Core rule 128"></span> ^cr-128

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 128 · PDF page 9" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 128 · PDF page 9</div></details>

A [[Card|card]] will always exist in one of the many [[Zone|Zones]] described during [[Setup]]. ^cr-128-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 128.1 · PDF page 9" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 128.1 · PDF page 9</div></details>

A [[Card|card]] will have a different [[Level|level]] of Privacy based on its state or where it resides at a given time. ^cr-128-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 128.2 · PDF page 9" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 128.2 · PDF page 9</div></details>

Unless specified otherwise by the state of the [[Card|card]], a card’s Privacy is the same as the [[Zone|zone]] it is located in. ^cr-128-2-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 128.2.a · PDF page 9" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 128.2.a · PDF page 9</div></details>

Secret: This [[Level|level]] of Privacy indicates that no player may read or look at the face of the [[Card|card]]. ^cr-128-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 128.3 · PDF page 9" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 128.3 · PDF page 9</div></details>

Private: This [[Level|level]] of Privacy indicates that only the controller of a [[Card|card]] on [[Board|the board]] or the owner of a card in any other [[Zone|zone]] may read or look at the face of the card. ^cr-128-4

**Example:** [[Card|Cards]] in a player's [[Hand|hand]] are owned by that player. Only that player may read or look at their faces.

**Example:** If a player controls a facedown [[Card|card]] at a [[Battlefield|battlefield]], that player and only that player may read or look at that card's face, regardless of who owns that card.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 128.4 · PDF page 9" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 128.4 · PDF page 9</div></details>

Public: This [[Level|level]] of Privacy indicates that any player may read or look at the face of the [[Card|card]]. ^cr-128-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 128.5 · PDF page 9" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 128.5 · PDF page 9</div></details>

A player cannot be compelled to perform an action on [[Card|cards]] whose privacy is secret or private if that action specifies a type or quality of card. ^cr-128-6

**Example:** A [[Card|card]] effect reads, “[[Play]] a [[Unit|unit]] from your [[Hand|hand]], ignoring its [[Costs|costs]].” Although the effect does not say “may”, the player can choose not to perform the action even if they have a unit in their hand, because the effect specifies a card type.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 128.6 · PDF page 9" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 128.6 · PDF page 9</div></details>

If an effect would compel a player to perform an action on [[Card|cards]] whose privacy is secret or private, and if that effect specifies a type or quality of card, the player in question may choose to ignore that instruction. Ignored [[Instructions|instructions]] are deemed impossible. ^cr-128-6-a
