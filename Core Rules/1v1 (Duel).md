<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 487.4 · PDF page 111" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 487.4 · PDF page 111</div></details>

Each player provides three (3) [[Battlefield|Battlefields]], included in their deck during deck building. Only 1 will be used, chosen during [[Setup|setup]]. ^cr-487-4-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 487.4.a · PDF page 111" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 487.4.a · PDF page 111</div></details>

[[Setup]]: Each player randomly selects one (1) of their three (3) [[Battlefield|Battlefields]]. The other two are removed and will not be used for this game. The selected Battlefields are placed simultaneously in the [[Battlefield Zone]]. ^cr-487-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 487.5 · PDF page 111" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 487.5 · PDF page 111</div></details>

Format: Best of 1. The first player to reach the [[Victory Score]] in [[Points]] wins the Match. ^cr-487-6

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 487.6 · PDF page 111" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 487.6 · PDF page 111</div></details>

First [[Turn]] Process: The player going second channels an extra [[Rune]] from their [[Rune Deck]] during their first [[Channel Phase]] of the game. ^cr-487-7
