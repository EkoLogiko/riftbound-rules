<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 489.4 · PDF page 112" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 489.4 · PDF page 112</div></details>

Each player provides three (3) [[Battlefield|Battlefields]], included in their deck during deck building. Only 1 will be used, chosen during [[Setup|setup]]. ^cr-489-4-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 489.4.a · PDF page 112" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 489.4.a · PDF page 112</div></details>

[[Setup]]: Each player randomly selects one (1) of their three (3) [[Battlefield|Battlefields]]. The other two are discarded and will not be used for this game. The selected Battlefields are placed simultaneously between the three Players before [[Play|play]] and will be used for this game. ^cr-489-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 489.5 · PDF page 112" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 489.5 · PDF page 112</div></details>

Format: Best of 1. The first player to reach the [[Victory Score]] in [[Points]] wins the Match. ^cr-489-6

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 489.6 · PDF page 112" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 489.6 · PDF page 112</div></details>

First [[Turn]] Process: The player going first does not [[Draw|draw]] a [[Card|card]] during their first [[Draw Phase]] of the game. The player going last channels an extra [[Rune]] from their [[Rune Deck]] during their first [[Channel Phase]] of the game. ^cr-489-7
