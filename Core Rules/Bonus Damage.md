# Bonus Damage

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Core Rules Index|Core rules]]

<span title="Core rule 712"></span> ^cr-712

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 712 · PDF page 116" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 712 · PDF page 116</div></details>

Bonus Damage is an intrinsic property that can be granted to [[Deal]] actions that influence the amount of [[Damage]] that the action is distributing. ^cr-713

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 713 · PDF page 116" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 713 · PDF page 116</div></details>

If more than one instance of Bonus Damage is applied or granted to a [[Deal]] action, all instances are summed and applied once. ^cr-714

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 714 · PDF page 116" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 714 · PDF page 116</div></details>

Bonus Damage can only be a positive value, and can only increase the amount of [[Damage]] being distributed. ^cr-714-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 714.1 · PDF page 117" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 714.1 · PDF page 117</div></details>

If, for any reason, Bonus Damage would be a negative number, then no Bonus Damage is applied to the action. ^cr-714-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 714.2 · PDF page 117" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 714.2 · PDF page 117</div></details>

Bonus Damage applies to the total [[Damage|damage]] Dealt by one instance of the action. ^cr-715

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 715 · PDF page 117" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 715 · PDF page 117</div></details>

If the [[Deal]] action has a single target, the amount of [[Damage]] to that target will be increased by the Bonus Damage granted to it. ^cr-715-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 715.1 · PDF page 117" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 715.1 · PDF page 117</div></details>

If the [[Deal]] action has multiple targets, the amount of [[Damage]] dealt to each target is increased by Bonus Damage individually and separately. ^cr-715-2

**Example:** Singularity is a [[Spell|spell]] that says “[[Deal]] 6 to each of up to two [[Unit|units]].” A player plays Singularity while they also [[Control|control]] Annie, Fiery, a unit that says “Your spells and [[Ability|abilities]] deal 1 Bonus Damage.” Singularity deals 1 Bonus Damage to both of its targets, dealing 7 to each.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 715.2 · PDF page 117" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 715.2 · PDF page 117</div></details>

If the [[Deal]] action Splits [[Damage|damage]], then the Bonus Damage applies to the amount of Damage that will be Split. This can alter the number of targets eligible to be chosen. ^cr-715-3

**Example:** Volibear, Furious is a [[Unit|unit]] that says in part “When I attack, [[Deal|deal]] 5 [[Damage|damage]] split among any number of [[Enemy|enemy]] units here.” A player attacks with Volibear, Furious while they also [[Control|control]] Annie, Fiery, a unit that says “Your [[Spell|spells]] and [[Ability|abilities]] deal 1 Bonus Damage.” Volibear, Furious now deals 6 damage split among any number of enemy units at its [[Location|location]], and can choose to split that damage among up to 6 units rather than the usual 5.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 715.3 · PDF page 117" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 715.3 · PDF page 117</div></details>

If no [[Damage|damage]] was Dealt, then Bonus Damage will not apply. ^cr-715-4

**Example:** Teemo, Strategist is a [[Unit|unit]] that reads in part “When I defend, choose an [[Enemy|enemy]] unit here and [[Reveal|reveal]] the top 5 [[Card|cards]] of your [[Main Deck]]. [[Deal]] 1 to that unit for each card with [[Hidden]] revealed this way, then [[Recycle|recycle]] the revealed cards.” He has Rabadon’s Deathcrown [[Attached|attached]] to him. An enemy unit moves to the [[Battlefield|battlefield]] where Teemo is located and a [[Combat|combat]] opens there. Teemo’s controller reveals the top 5 cards of their Main Deck and reveals no cards with Hidden. Although the [[Ability|ability]] has 3 Bonus Damage from Rabadon’s Deathcrown, no deal action is performed for the Bonus Damage to apply to.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 715.4 · PDF page 117" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 715.4 · PDF page 117</div></details>

If [[Damage]] is replaced or reduced by any means, the replacing or reducing action will include the Bonus Damage in the total damage when determining how much damage is to be dealt. ^cr-715-4-a

**Example:** A [[Unit|unit]] has [[Prevent|prevent]] 3 applied to them. The unit is located at the Void Gate [[Battlefield|battlefield]]. An opponent targets the unit with Hextech Ray. Hextech Ray deals 4 [[Damage|damage]] to the unit, including the Bonus Damage from Void Gate, which the prevent effect will prevent 3 of. The unit takes 1 damage.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 715.4.a · PDF page 117" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 715.4.a · PDF page 117</div></details>
