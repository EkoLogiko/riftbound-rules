# Reveal

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Core Rules Index|Core rules]]

<span title="Core rule 425"></span> ^cr-425

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425 · PDF page 84" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425 · PDF page 84</div></details>

Revealing is the act of presenting a [[Card|card]] to all players from a [[Zone|zone]] that one or more players do not have access to the information of. ^cr-425-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.1 · PDF page 84" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.1 · PDF page 84</div></details>

Revealed is a [[Temporary|temporary]] state and is not a [[Zone|zone]]. ^cr-425-1-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.1.a · PDF page 84" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.1.a · PDF page 84</div></details>

Other [[Card|cards]], including the card being revealed, can reference the act of being Revealed. ^cr-425-1-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.1.a.1 · PDF page 84" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.1.a.1 · PDF page 84</div></details>

[[Card|Cards]] remain in the [[Zone|zone]] they are being Revealed from. ^cr-425-1-a-2

**Example:** If a [[Card|card]] is being Revealed from the top of a player's [[Main Deck]], it is still the top card of that player's Main Deck.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.1.a.2 · PDF page 84" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.1.a.2 · PDF page 84</div></details>

The state of being Revealed lasts for the duration specified by the [[Game Effect|game effect]] that applied the state; if there is no duration specified and the state was caused by a Reveal action as instructed by a [[Spell|spell]] or [[Ability|ability]], it lasts until the resolution of that spell or ability finishes. ^cr-425-1-a-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.1.a.3 · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.1.a.3 · PDF page 85</div></details>

Unless otherwise described, Revealed [[Card|cards]] do nothing else beyond become temporarily known information to all players. ^cr-425-1-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.1.b · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.1.b · PDF page 85</div></details>

Revealing is a [[Limited Action]]. ^cr-425-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.2 · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.2 · PDF page 85</div></details>

Players may only Reveal [[Card|cards]] from Private or Secret [[Zone|zones]] when instructed to do so by [[Game Effect|Game Effects]]. ^cr-425-2-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.2.a · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.2.a · PDF page 85</div></details>

During the course of a game of Riftbound, a player may choose to show [[Private Information|Private information]] to one or more other players. This does not count as revealing and does not trigger any effects that trigger when [[Card|cards]] are revealed. ^cr-425-2-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.2.b · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.2.b · PDF page 85</div></details>

This action is formatted as "Reveal [[Card|cards]] from &#91;[[Zone|zone]]&#93;." or “Reveal &#91;Zone&#93;.” ^cr-425-3

**Example:** "Reveal 2 [[Card|cards]] from the top of your [[Main Deck]]" would be executed by taking the two cards from the top of your Main Deck, and then presenting them to all players to clearly read and understand at the table. Then, when all players have had a chance to understand the revealed information, return them to the top of the Main Deck in the same order.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.3 · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.3 · PDF page 85</div></details>

When the [[Zone|zone]] is instructed to be Revealed without indicating a number of [[Card|cards]], that refers to “All cards currently in the specified zone.” ^cr-425-3-a

**Example:** A [[Card|card]] reads “Choose an opponent and reveal your [[Hand|hand]]. That player picks a card in your hand. You may [[Play|play]] the rest this [[Turn|turn]], ignoring their [[Costs|costs]].” When instructed to reveal their hand, that player reveals the cards currently in their hand.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.3.a · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.3.a · PDF page 85</div></details>

The Revealed state is applied to those [[Card|cards]]. Any further cards added to that [[Zone|zone]] after the instruction has been executed do not become Revealed. ^cr-425-3-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.3.a.1 · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.3.a.1 · PDF page 85</div></details>

[[Game Effect|Game Effects]] can manipulate or modify the [[Card|cards]] Revealed while they are Revealed ^cr-425-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.4 · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.4 · PDF page 85</div></details>

While [[Card|cards]] are Revealed, those cards can be further manipulated, or accessed. They can even have their destination modified for when the Reveal ends. ^cr-425-4-a

**Example:** "Reveal 2 [[Card|cards]] from the top of your [[Main Deck]]. Put one into your [[Hand|hand]] and [[Recycle]] the other" would be executed by taking the top two cards from your Main Deck, presenting them to the table, and once every player present has had a chance to understand them you would make the selection of one of them to add to your hand. The one you did not select would then be Recycled to the bottom of the Main Deck.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 425.4.a · PDF page 85" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 425.4.a · PDF page 85</div></details>
