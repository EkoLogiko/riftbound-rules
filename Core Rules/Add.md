# Add

<span title="Core rule 430"></span> ^cr-430

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 430 · PDF page 88" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 430 · PDF page 88</div></details>

Adding is the action of putting resources into a player's [[Rune Pool]]. ^cr-430-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 430.1 · PDF page 88" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 430.1 · PDF page 88</div></details>

Triggered and [[Activated Ability|activated abilities]] that Add resources finalize immediately when they are played, even when handling [[Task|tasks]] or during the resolution of [[Spell|spells]] and [[Ability|abilities]], and resolve as soon as they are finalized. ^cr-430-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 430.2 · PDF page 88" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 430.2 · PDF page 88</div></details>

[[Priority and Focus]] will not pass from Add [[Ability|abilities]] being finalized or resolving. Any ongoing [[Task]] or step in the FEPR process is paused while they are being finalized and while they are resolving. ^cr-430-2-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 430.2.a · PDF page 88" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 430.2.a · PDF page 88</div></details>

[[Spell|Spells]] that Add resources will be finalized and will resolve at the normal timing for spells. ^cr-430-2-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 430.2.b · PDF page 88" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 430.2.b · PDF page 88</div></details>

[[Activated Ability|Activated abilities]] that Add resources and have the Reaction [[Tag|tag]] can be activated at any time that [[Spell|spells]] or [[Ability|abilities]] require resources be paid. ^cr-430-3

**Example:** A player can add [[Energy]] and [[Power]] through any means before initiating the process of playing a [[Spell|spell]]. After initiating that process, in the Pay [[Costs]] step, they may activate Add Reactions to add Energy or Power to pay costs.

**Example:** A player moves two [[Unit|units]] to a [[Battlefield|battlefield]] where their opponent controls a Mageseeker Investigator. Although they have no window of [[Priority|priority]], they may activate Add Reactions to pay for the [[Applied Cost|applied cost]] from Mageseeker Investigator, and those [[Ability|abilities]] finalize and resolve immediately.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 430.3 · PDF page 88" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 430.3 · PDF page 88</div></details>

Adding is a [[Limited Action]]. ^cr-430-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 430.4 · PDF page 88" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 430.4 · PDF page 88</div></details>

Players may only Add resources when [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] direct them to do so. ^cr-430-4-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 430.4.a · PDF page 88" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 430.4.a · PDF page 88</div></details>

This action is formatted as "Add &#91;one or more resources&#93;." e.g., "Add &#91;2&#93;." means "Add 2 [[Energy]]." e.g., "&#91;E&#93;: Add &#91;Y&#93;." means "Add 1 [[Power]] of the Order [[Domain|domain]]." e.g., "Add &#91;1&#93;&#91;G&#93;." means "Add 1 Energy and 1 Power of the Calm domain." ^cr-430-5
