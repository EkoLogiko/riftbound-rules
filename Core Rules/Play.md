<span title="Core rule 420"></span> ^cr-420

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420 · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420 · PDF page 82</div></details>

A player Plays [[Card|cards]] by placing them on the [[Chain|chain]] and queuing them to be finalized. ^cr-420-1

See [[Playing Cards#^cr-349|Playing Cards]] for more information on [[Playing Cards|playing cards]].

See [[Handling Tasks and Resolving Chain Items#^cr-337|Finalize]] for more information on finalizing [[Card|cards]].

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.1 · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.1 · PDF page 82</div></details>

By default, a player can only Play [[Card|cards]] from their [[Hand|hand]] or their [[Champion Zone|Chosen Champion zone]]. ^cr-420-1-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.1.a · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.1.a · PDF page 82</div></details>

This is a [[Discretionary Action]]. ^cr-420-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.2 · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.2 · PDF page 82</div></details>

As long as a player has the resources to pay the [[Costs|costs]] associated with the [[Card|card]] and legal choices to make for their cards, they may Play cards. ^cr-420-2-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.2.a · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.2.a · PDF page 82</div></details>

[[Game Effect|Game effects]] may result in [[Card|cards]] being Played as part of their resolution. ^cr-420-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.3 · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.3 · PDF page 82</div></details>

This treats Play as a [[Limited Action]]. ^cr-420-3-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.3.a · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.3.a · PDF page 82</div></details>

Treat all steps of Play as normal, except as noted by the [[Game Effect|game effect]] instructing this Limited [[Play Effect]]. ^cr-420-3-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.3.b · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.3.b · PDF page 82</div></details>

Such [[Game Effect|game effects]] may specify choices or effects such as alternate [[Costs|costs]], ignored costs, play [[Location|locations]], targets, or other choices or effects normally made or processed during Finalization. ^cr-420-3-b-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.3.b.1 · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.3.b.1 · PDF page 82</div></details>

Such [[Game Effect|game effects]] may specify those choices or effects based on the condition of [[Board|the board]]. If they do so, that information is checked on finalization of the resulting [[Game Object]], unless it is specified as being checked earlier. ^cr-420-3-b-2

**Example:** Bone Skewer is a [[Spell|spell]] that reads in part “Choose a [[Battlefield|battlefield]]. An opponent reveals their [[Hand|hand]]. You may choose a [[Unit|unit]] from it. They play that unit to that battlefield, ignoring any and all [[Costs|costs]].” The chosen battlefield isn’t checked on finalization of the resulting [[Game Object|game object]] but when the spell itself is resolving.

**Example:** A [[Spell|spell]] reads in part “Play a [[Unit|unit]] from your [[Trash|trash]] to an [[Enemy|enemy]] [[Occupied Battlefield|occupied battlefield]], ignoring its [[Energy Cost|Energy cost]].” On finalization, they can choose any enemy occupied battlefield to play the unit to. If no such [[Battlefield|battlefield]] exists as the unit is being finalized, it will fail to finalize.

**Example:** Rek’Sai, Swarm Queen is a [[Unit|unit]] that reads “When I attack, you may [[Reveal|reveal]] the top 2 [[Card|cards]] of your [[Main Deck]]. You may [[Banish|banish]] one, then play it. If it is a unit, you may play it here. [[Recycle]] the rest.” The [[Attack Trigger|attack trigger]] adds a permission to the normal valid [[Location|locations]] for the played unit, that being “here.” The “here” in Rek’Sai’s [[Ability|ability]] is a referent, whose information is checked when the ability resolves. As such, it will not check on finalization of the unit being played.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.3.b.2 · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.3.b.2 · PDF page 82</div></details>

If there are no eligible [[Card|cards]] to Play when instructed to Play in this manner, then nothing happens and resolution continues. ^cr-420-3-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.3.c · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.3.c · PDF page 82</div></details>

Some [[Ability|Abilities]] trigger when [[Card|cards]] are played or otherwise check whether cards have been played. ^cr-420-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.4 · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.4 · PDF page 82</div></details>

Any such [[Triggered Ability|triggered abilities]] trigger when the act of playing the [[Card|card]] has been completed by the resolution of the card. ^cr-420-4-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.4.a · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.4.a · PDF page 82</div></details>

If a [[Game Effect|game effect]] prevents the resolution of the [[Card|card]]—for example, because the card was countered—[[Ability|abilities]] that trigger on [[Playing Cards|playing cards]] will not trigger. ^cr-420-4-a-1

See [[Counter#^cr-426|Counter]] for more information.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.4.a.1 · PDF page 82" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.4.a.1 · PDF page 82</div></details>

Non-triggered [[Ability|abilities]] and conditional clauses that check [[Card|cards]] being played do so by means of referencing whether said cards have been Finalized. ^cr-420-4-b

**Example:** A player plays a [[Spell|spell]], which is countered by Defy. Any [[Legion]] [[Ability|abilities]] of [[Game Object|game objects]] controlled by that same player will be active.

**Example:** A player plays a [[Spell|spell]], which is countered by Defy. If that player plays Battering Ram and has played no other [[Card|cards]] that [[Turn|turn]], it will [[Costs|cost]] &#91;4&#93; [[Energy]].

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 420.4.b · PDF page 82-83" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 420.4.b · PDF page 82-83</div></details>

If an effect refers to a [[Card|card]] being played and doesn’t do so by means of checking whether cards were played, it refers to the card being added to the [[Chain|chain]] and queued for Finalization, that is, the Play [[Game Action]]. ^cr-420-5

**Example:** Astral Heron is a [[Unit|unit]] that reads “When you play your first [[Card|card]] each [[Turn|turn]], if I'm at a [[Battlefield|battlefield]], the next card you play this turn [[Costs|costs]] &#91;2&#93;&#91;A&#93;&#91;A&#93; less.” This effect applies a discount to the next card the play [[Game Action|game action]], discretionary or limited, is performed on.
