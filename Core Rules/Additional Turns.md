<span title="Core rule 734"></span> ^cr-734

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 734 · PDF page 121" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 734 · PDF page 121</div></details>

Certain [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] will instruct a player to “take a [[Turn|turn]] after this.” These effects create a [[Temporary|temporary]] Additional Turn owned by that player that is inserted into the turn queue after the current turn. ^cr-735

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 735 · PDF page 121" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 735 · PDF page 121</div></details>

[[Turn]] order is established when the game begins as a repeating set of players. This populates a looping queue of turns that each player will take, starting with the first turn taken by the First Player, and repeating indefinitely. ^cr-736

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 736 · PDF page 121" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 736 · PDF page 121</div></details>

When an Additional [[Turn]] is inserted into this queue, it does not change the Turn Order of the game. The owner of the Additional Turn just has the next queued turn. After that turn is completed, it will be removed and the queue will proceed with its previously queued turns. ^cr-737

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 737 · PDF page 121" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 737 · PDF page 121</div></details>

If multiple Additional Turns are queued, they are added to the queue in the order the [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] that generated them occurred. ^cr-738

**Example:** The First Player plays, through some means, two Time Warps during their [[Turn|turn]]. The Time Warps create two Additional Turns for their controller and insert them into the turn queue after the current turn. If the turn queue is represented as &#91;> A > B > C > D >&#93;, then these Additional Turns will appear as &#91;> A > A* > A* > B > C > D >&#93;. After the last Additional Turn is played, the queue returns to its previously queued turns. The “*” denotes that a turn is an Additional Turn.

**Example:** The First Player plays Promising Future during their [[Turn|turn]], during the resolution of which the Second and Fourth Player choose, [[Banish|banish]], and [[Play|play]] one Time Warp each. The Fourth Player’s Time Warp resolves first, inserting an Additional Turn for them in the queue as such: &#91;> A > D* > B > C > D >&#93;. The Second Player’s Time Warp resolves afterwards, inserting that turn: &#91;> A > B* > D* > B > C > D >&#93;. When the First Player passes the turn, the Second Player will take their turn, followed by the Fourth Player, after which the queue returns to its previously queued turns.
