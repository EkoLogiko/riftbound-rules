# Burn Out

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Core Rules Index|Core rules]]

<span title="Core rule 432"></span> ^cr-432

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432 · PDF page 89" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432 · PDF page 89</div></details>

Burning Out is an action a player must perform if they attempt to [[Move|move]] one or more [[Card|cards]] from their [[Main Deck]] to any other [[Zone|zone]] in excess of the number of cards remaining in their Main Deck: ^cr-432-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.1 · PDF page 89" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.1 · PDF page 89</div></details>

If a player must [[Draw]] [[Card|cards]] in excess to the number of cards in their [[Main Deck]], they will Draw as many as possible, perform this action, then Draw the remaining amount instructed. ^cr-432-1-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.1.a · PDF page 89" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.1.a · PDF page 89</div></details>

If a player must put one or more [[Card|cards]] from their [[Main Deck]] in any other [[Zone|zone]], such as the [[Trash]], in excess of the number of cards in their deck they will do so as much as possible, perform this action, and then complete the remaining number required by the instruction. ^cr-432-1-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.1.b · PDF page 89" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.1.b · PDF page 89</div></details>

If an instruction directs a player to look at or [[Reveal|reveal]] [[Card|cards]] in excess to the number of cards in a player’s [[Main Deck]], that player looks at or Reveals as many as possible, but does not Burn Out, then proceeds with the rest of the instruction. ^cr-432-1-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.1.c · PDF page 89" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.1.c · PDF page 89</div></details>

If there are insufficient [[Card|cards]] among the looked at or [[Reveal|revealed]] cards to perform subsequent actions to the revealed or looked at cards, any further [[Instructions|instructions]] are negated. This does not cause a Burn Out, even if those instructions would cause those cards to change [[Zone|zones]]. ^cr-432-1-c-1

Reminder: [[Card|Cards]] are considered in the [[Zone|zone]] of origin while being looked at or [[Reveal|revealed]], in this case the [[Main Deck]].

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.1.c.1 · PDF page 89" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.1.c.1 · PDF page 89</div></details>

To Burn Out, a player does the following in sequence: ^cr-432-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.2 · PDF page 89" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.2 · PDF page 89</div></details>

Performs as much of the prescribed action as possible. ^cr-432-2-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.2.a · PDF page 89" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.2.a · PDF page 89</div></details>

Recycles their [[Trash|trash]] into their [[Main Deck]]. ^cr-432-2-b

Reminder: When multiple [[Card|cards]] are Recycled to the [[Main Deck]] at the same time, those cards must be randomized

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.2.b · PDF page 89-90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.2.b · PDF page 89-90</div></details>

Chooses an opponent to gain 1 point. ^cr-432-2-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.2.c · PDF page 90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.2.c · PDF page 90</div></details>

Completes the remainder of the action that caused them to burn out. ^cr-432-2-d

**Example:** A player attempts to [[Draw|draw]] 1 during their [[Draw Phase]] while their [[Main Deck]] is empty. That player instead recycles their [[Trash|trash]] into their Main Deck, randomizing it as normal, then chooses an opponent to gain 1 point, and then draws 1.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.2.d · PDF page 90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.2.d · PDF page 90</div></details>

A player's [[Main Deck]] may remain empty as they Burn Out, usually because their [[Trash|trash]] is also empty. When they attempt to perform the original action again, it will cause another Burn Out. ^cr-432-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.3 · PDF page 90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.3 · PDF page 90</div></details>

Unless some effect intervenes, this will result in them burning out repeatedly, giving 1 point to an opponent each time, until an opponent passes the [[Victory Score]] and wins the game. ^cr-432-3-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.3.a · PDF page 90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.3.a · PDF page 90</div></details>

[[Points]] gained after the first Burn Out being processed in sequence cannot be replaced or prevented by any means. ^cr-432-3-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.3.b · PDF page 90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.3.b · PDF page 90</div></details>

[[Points]] gained after the first Burn Out being processed in sequence that cause a player to reach or surpass the [[Victory Score]] for their game mode will cause that player to win the game if they also have more points than every opponent. ^cr-432-3-c

**Example:** If a player has no [[Card|cards]] in their deck or their [[Trash|trash]] and they go to [[Draw|draw]] 1, they will begin to burn out repeatedly. After the first burn out is processed, any subsequent burnout that brings a player to a number of [[Points|points]] greater than the [[Victory Score]] and more than any opponent will cause them to win the game.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.3.c · PDF page 90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.3.c · PDF page 90</div></details>

The player wins immediately, without needing to wait for a [[Cleanups|cleanup]] to occur. ^cr-432-3-c-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.3.c.1 · PDF page 90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.3.c.1 · PDF page 90</div></details>

Burning Out is a [[Limited Action]]. ^cr-432-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.4 · PDF page 90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.4 · PDF page 90</div></details>

Players may only burn out when [[Game Effect|Game Effects]] direct them to do so. ^cr-432-4-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 432.4.a · PDF page 90" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 432.4.a · PDF page 90</div></details>

Burning Out is a [[Replacement Effect]]. ^cr-432-5

See [[Replacement Effect#^cr-367|Replacement Effects]] for more information.
