Each player provides three (3) [[Battlefield|Battlefields]], included in their deck during deck building. ^cr-490-4-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 490.4.a · PDF page 112" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 490.4.a · PDF page 112</div></details>

The player who is taking the first [[Turn|turn]] removes their [[Battlefield|Battlefields]]. They will not be used. ^cr-490-4-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 490.4.b · PDF page 112" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 490.4.b · PDF page 112</div></details>

[[Setup]]: Each player who is not going first randomly selects one (1) of their three (3) [[Battlefield|Battlefields]]. The other two are removed and will not be used for this game. The selected Battlefields are placed simultaneously between the players before [[Play|play]] and will be used for this game. ^cr-490-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 490.5 · PDF page 112-113" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 490.5 · PDF page 112-113</div></details>

Format: Best of 1. The first player to reach the [[Victory Score]] in [[Points]] wins the Match. ^cr-490-6

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 490.6 · PDF page 113" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 490.6 · PDF page 113</div></details>

First [[Turn]] Process: The player going first does not [[Draw|draw]] a [[Card|card]] during their first [[Draw Phase]] of the game. The player going last channels an extra [[Rune]] from their [[Rune Deck]] during their first [[Channel Phase]] of the game. ^cr-490-7
