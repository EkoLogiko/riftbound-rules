# Ownership

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Core Rules Index|Core rules]]

<span title="Core rule 127"></span> ^cr-127

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 127 · PDF page 9" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 127 · PDF page 9</div></details>

For gameplay purposes, a [[Card|card]]'s Owner is the player who brought it into the game, either as their [[Champion Legend]], one of their [[Battlefield|Battlefields]], as part of their [[Main Deck]] or [[Rune Deck]], or Created during [[Process of Playing a Card|the process of play]]. The legal owner of a card doesn't matter during gameplay. ^cr-127-1

**Example:** Bo didn't bring a deck, so Alice loans him a deck. During the game, Bo is considered the owner of those [[Card|cards]], even though legally they belong to Alice.
