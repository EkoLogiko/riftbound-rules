Spell is a [[Card|card]] type. ^cr-154

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 154 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 154 · PDF page 22</div></details>

A spell can be played during an [[Open State]] outside of [[Showdowns]] on its controller's [[Turn|turn]]. ^cr-155

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 155 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 155 · PDF page 22</div></details>

A spell is controlled by the player who played it. ^cr-156

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 156 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 156 · PDF page 22</div></details>

A spell creates a [[Game Effect|game effect]] according to its [[Instructions|instructions]] and is then placed in the [[Trash]] of the player who owns it. ^cr-157

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 157 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 157 · PDF page 22</div></details>

When a spell is successfully played, a player executes the [[Rules Text|rules text]] of the spell. This is called Resolving the spell. ^cr-158

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 158 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 158 · PDF page 22</div></details>

Spells have their [[Rules Text|rules text]] executed from top to bottom when they are Resolved. ^cr-158-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 158.1 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 158.1 · PDF page 22</div></details>

If a later part of a spell applies a [[Replacement Effect]] that alters earlier parts of the spell, apply those replacement effects as appropriate. ^cr-158-2

**Example:** A spell says "Choose a [[Unit|unit]]. [[Kill]] it the next time it takes [[Damage|damage]] this [[Turn|turn]]." and "&#91;[[Legion]]&#93; — Kill it now instead." If the Legion condition is satisfied, the unit is killed immediately and the instruction to kill it the next time it takes damage is negated, even if the unit remains on [[Board|the board]] somehow.

See [[Replacement Effect#^cr-367|Replacement Effects]] for more information.

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 158.2 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 158.2 · PDF page 23</div></details>

While a spell or [[Ability|ability]] on the [[Chain|chain]] is Resolving, no other spells or abilities can be finalized on the chain or resolved, including [[Triggered Ability|triggered abilities]] or [[Game Effect|game effects]] that would occur as a result of the execution of the spell. ^cr-158-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 158.3 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 158.3 · PDF page 23</div></details>

Finish resolving all effects of a spell before addressing any [[Chain|chain]] items or [[Task|Tasks]] the spell may have added or made outstanding through execution. ^cr-158-3-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 158.3.a · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 158.3.a · PDF page 23</div></details>

Certain [[Keywords]] on spells are not executable [[Rules Text|rules text]], but instead intrinsic properties of the spell. ^cr-159

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Core rule 159 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Core rule 159 · PDF page 23</div></details>

These determine inherent properties and behaviors of the spell before being played or while on the [[Chain|chain]]. ^cr-159-1
