### Round Structures

<span title="Tournament rule 202.2"></span> ^tr-202-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 202.2 · PDF page 2" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 202.2 · PDF page 2</div></details>

[[Swiss]]: A competition or component of a competition where players are paired each round against players they haven’t already played in the competition who, if possible, share the same current record. ^tr-202-2-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 202.2.a · PDF page 2" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 202.2.a · PDF page 2</div></details>

[[Power Pairing]]: When the final round of a [[Swiss|swiss]] competition is paired in exact order of the current standings of the competition. For example: Seed 1 plays against seed 2, seed 3 plays against seed 4, etc. ^tr-202-2-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 202.2.a.1 · PDF page 2" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 202.2.a.1 · PDF page 2</div></details>

[[Playoff]]: A competition or component of a competition where players are initially paired based on their standing and are subsequently eliminated by losses. ^tr-202-2-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 202.2.b · PDF page 2" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 202.2.b · PDF page 2</div></details>

Multiple: Some competitions may consist of multiple formats and/or structures within the same competition. ^tr-202-3
