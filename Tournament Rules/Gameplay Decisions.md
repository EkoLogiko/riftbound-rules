# Gameplay Decisions

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Tournament Rules|Tournament rules]]

<span title="Tournament rule 509"></span> ^tr-509

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509 · PDF page 23</div></details>

In competitions, players are responsible for considering options before taking actions. ^tr-509-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.1 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.1 · PDF page 23</div></details>

Once an action is communicated to an opponent, that action can’t generally be taken back. ^tr-509-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.2 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.2 · PDF page 23</div></details>

At [[Casual OPL|low OPL]], a player can reverse their most recent action and make a different decision for that action (including not taking it at all) as long as no new action has been taken by either player. The change in decision does not need to be immediate. ^tr-509-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.3 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.3 · PDF page 23</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], a player can reverse their most recent action and make a different decision for that action (including not taking it at all) if no new action has been taken by either player and the following is true: ^tr-509-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.4 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.4 · PDF page 23</div></details>

The player wants to make a different decision for a [[Spell|spell]] or [[Ability|ability]] immediately after all choices have been made and communicated for putting it onto the [[Chain|chain]]. ^tr-509-4-a

Example:

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.4.a · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.4.a · PDF page 23</div></details>

A player plays Discipline indicating a [[Unit|unit]]. They immediately change their mind and choose a different unit, or choose not to [[Play|play]] the Discipline at all. ^tr-509-4-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.4.a.1 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.4.a.1 · PDF page 23</div></details>

The player wants to make a different decision for playing a [[Unit|unit]] or [[Gear|gear]] immediately after playing it to a [[Location|location]]. Example: ^tr-509-4-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.4.b · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.4.b · PDF page 23</div></details>

A player plays Traveling Merchant to Reaver’s Row. They immediately change their mind and choose to [[Play|play]] it to their [[Base|base]] instead, or choose not to play it at all. ^tr-509-4-b-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.4.b.1 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.4.b.1 · PDF page 23</div></details>

The player wants to make a different decision immediately after making a choice such as [[Movement|moving]] a [[Unit|unit]] to a [[Location|location]] or discarding a [[Card|card]]. Example: ^tr-509-4-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.4.c · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.4.c · PDF page 23</div></details>

A player moves Pouty Poro to Void Gate. They immediately change their mind and choose to [[Move|move]] an additional [[Unit|unit]] there as well, or they decide not to move at all. ^tr-509-4-c-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.4.c.1 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.4.c.1 · PDF page 23</div></details>

Players can always [[Ready|ready]] [[Rune|runes]] they just [[Exhaust|exhausted]] for [[Energy]] or unrecycle runes they just recycled for [[Power]] as long as no new action has been taken and [[Focus|focus]] has not been passed. ^tr-509-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.5 · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.5 · PDF page 23</div></details>

When changing decisions as described in [[Gameplay Decisions#^tr-509-3|Gameplay Decisions]]. - [[Gameplay Decisions#^tr-509-4|Gameplay Decisions]]., players can [[Ready|ready]]/unrecycle [[Rune|runes]] used for those decisions. ^tr-509-5-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.5.a · PDF page 23" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.5.a · PDF page 23</div></details>

Example: A player exhausts two [[Rune|runes]] and plays Pouty Poro from [[Hand|hand]] to their [[Base|base]], then immediately decides they do not want to [[Play|play]] it. They may return it to their hand and [[Ready|ready]] those two runes. ^tr-509-5-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.5.a.1 · PDF page 24" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.5.a.1 · PDF page 24</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], flexibility on gameplay decisions is not intended as a tool to allow players to reverse decisions after an opponent is required to indicate information that reveals that decision to be a tactical error. ^tr-509-6

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.6 · PDF page 24" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.6 · PDF page 24</div></details>

Example: Player A moves their 3 [[Might]] [[Unit|unit]] to a [[Battlefield|battlefield]] and passes [[Focus|focus]]. Player B indicates their 2 Might unit at that battlefield is now at 4 Might due to their [[Legend|legend]], Wuju Bladesman. Player A can no longer change their decision to [[Move|move]] to that battlefield and pass focus. ^tr-509-6-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.6.a · PDF page 24" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.6.a · PDF page 24</div></details>

Decisions made based on inaccurately represented [[Public Information|public information]] may still be rewound at the discretion of the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. Inaccurately representing information to try to get a [[Rewinding|rewind]] is [[Cheating|cheating]]. ^tr-509-6-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.6.b · PDF page 24" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.6.b · PDF page 24</div></details>

Intentionally attempting to reverse decisions to gain information from an opponent is considered [[Angle Shooting|angle shooting]]. Players may not change their decisions based on an opponent’s physical reaction or as a response to an opponent starting to take a [[Game Action|game action]]. See [[Unsporting Conduct#^tr-704-4|Angle Shooting]] for more information on angle shooting. ^tr-509-7

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 509.7 · PDF page 24" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 509.7 · PDF page 24</div></details>

While changing decisions as described above is allowed, players must still maintain an appropriate pace of [[Play|play]]. See [[Tournament Errors#^tr-703-2|Slow Play]] for more information on [[Slow Play]]. ^tr-509-8
