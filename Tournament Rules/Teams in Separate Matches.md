# Teams in Separate Matches

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Tournament Rules|Tournament rules]]

<span title="Tournament rule 507"></span> ^tr-507

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 507 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 507 · PDF page 22</div></details>

Players may communicate with teammates at all times unless explicitly prohibited by the format rules. ^tr-507-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 507.1 · PDF page 22" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 507.1 · PDF page 22</div></details>

Players that acquire secret or [[Private Information|private information]] about a match in which a teammate is playing can’t communicate with that teammate for the remainder of that match. See [[Privacy#^cr-128|Privacy]] for information types. ^tr-507-2
