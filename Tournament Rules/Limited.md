### Legality

<span title="Tournament rule 602.1"></span> ^tr-602-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.1 · PDF page 25" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.1 · PDF page 25</div></details>

A [[Limited Competition|limited competition]] is one where all product needed for [[Play|play]] is provided during the competition. ^tr-602-1-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.1.a · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.1.a · PDF page 26</div></details>

A [[Card|card]] may only be included in a limited deck if it is provided to the player as part of the competition. ^tr-602-1-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.1.a.1 · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.1.a.1 · PDF page 26</div></details>

For some limited competitions, players may be given additional promotional [[Card|cards]] that the [[Head Judge|head judge]] will specify are not part of that player’s [[Limited Card Pool|limited card pool]] and can’t be used for that event. ^tr-602-1-a-1-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.1.a.1.a · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.1.a.1.a · PDF page 26</div></details>

Players may utilize any number of the 6 [[Basic Runes|basic Runes]] from any source, even if they are not in the player’s provided [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]]. ^tr-602-1-a-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.1.a.2 · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.1.a.2 · PDF page 26</div></details>

Players may utilize as many “blank” [[Battlefield|battlefields]] as they need for limited gameplay. See [[Limited#^tr-602-3-d|Limited]] below for more information on “blank” battlefields. ^tr-602-1-a-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.1.a.3 · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.1.a.3 · PDF page 26</div></details>

Even if a [[Card|card]] is banned in [[Constructed|constructed]], it is legal to use in a limited deck as long as it is not also banned in that limited format. ^tr-602-1-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.1.b · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.1.b · PDF page 26</div></details>

### Abnormal Product

<span title="Tournament rule 602.2"></span> ^tr-602-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.2 · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.2 · PDF page 26</div></details>

There is no guarantee that the composition or distribution of any specific unopened product is correct. ^tr-602-2-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.2.a · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.2.a · PDF page 26</div></details>

If a player notices a discrepancy between expected rarity or frequency of [[Card|cards]] in a pack and what is opened, they must notify a judge. ^tr-602-2-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.2.b · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.2.b · PDF page 26</div></details>

The [[Head Judge|head judge]] may, at their discretion, replace atypical product in these cases. ^tr-602-2-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.2.c · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.2.c · PDF page 26</div></details>

In some cases, competition officials may allow players to provide their own product, but in such cases player-brought products must be pooled and distributed amongst participants randomly. ^tr-602-2-d

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.2.d · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.2.d · PDF page 26</div></details>

### Building From a Limited Pool

<span title="Tournament rule 602.3"></span> ^tr-602-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3 · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3 · PDF page 26</div></details>

The rules for constructing a Riftbound deck are found in [[Deck Construction#^cr-101|Deck Construction]], except where modified here below for limited formats. ^tr-602-3-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.a · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.a · PDF page 26</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], players may not use [[Electronic Devices|electronic devices]] to take notes or as a strategic aid during deckbuilding. At [[Casual OPL|low OPL]], this can be allowed by the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. ^tr-602-3-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.b · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.b · PDF page 26</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], players may not receive any [[Outside Assistance|outside assistance]] during deckbuilding. At [[Casual OPL|low OPL]], this can be allowed by the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. ^tr-602-3-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.c · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.c · PDF page 26</div></details>

Limited decks can use [[Battlefield|battlefields]] from their provided [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]] or any number of "blank" battlefields. Players may opt to use “blank” battlefields in place of battlefields they received in their provided card pool. ^tr-602-3-d

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.d · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.d · PDF page 26</div></details>

“Blank” [[Battlefield|battlefields]] function as battlefields that do not have any [[Rules Text|rules text]]. Otherwise, they adhere to all other normal battlefield rules as defined in the Core Rules. ^tr-602-3-d-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.d.1 · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.d.1 · PDF page 26</div></details>

At [[Casual OPL|low OPL]], “Blank” [[Battlefield|battlefields]] can be represented by anything agreed upon by all players in the game. ^tr-602-3-d-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.d.2 · PDF page 26" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.d.2 · PDF page 26</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], “Blank” [[Battlefield|battlefields]] must be represented by any official Riftbound [[Card|card]], put in an opaque card sleeve with the cardback facing outwards. ^tr-602-3-d-2-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.d.2.a · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.d.2.a · PDF page 27</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], players must register a starting configuration from the [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]] provided and must register the remaining contents of their card pool as their [[Sideboard|sideboard]]. ^tr-602-3-e

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.e · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.e · PDF page 27</div></details>

Players must use their registered starting configuration in the first game of each match. ^tr-602-3-e-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.e.1 · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.e.1 · PDF page 27</div></details>

[[Legend]], [[Chosen Champion]], and [[Rune Deck|Rune deck]] composition must be registered as part of a player’s starting configuration. ^tr-602-3-e-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.e.2 · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.e.2 · PDF page 27</div></details>

For “best of 1” competitions, players must register three [[Battlefield|battlefields]] to randomly select from during the [[Start of Game Procedure|start of game procedure]], including any “blank” battlefields they wish to use. ^tr-602-3-e-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.e.3 · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.e.3 · PDF page 27</div></details>

For “best of 3” competitions, players may select any [[Battlefield|battlefield]] from their [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]] or a “blank” battlefield during the [[Start of Game Procedure|start of game procedure]]. ^tr-602-3-e-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.e.4 · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.e.4 · PDF page 27</div></details>

Players may only use a specific [[Copy|copy]] of a [[Battlefield|battlefield]] once per match, but may use the same named battlefield as many times as they have copies of it in their provided [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]]. ^tr-602-3-e-4-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.e.4.a · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.e.4.a · PDF page 27</div></details>

At [[Casual OPL|low OPL]], starting configuration registration is not required and players may freely change the composition of their starting deck and [[Sideboard|sideboard]] between games and matches unless otherwise specified by the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. Players may still only use a specific [[Copy|copy]] of a [[Battlefield|battlefield]] once per match, but may use the same named battlefield as many times as they have copies of it in their provided [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]]. ^tr-602-3-f

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.f · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.f · PDF page 27</div></details>

The [[Sideboard|sideboard]] process described in [[Start of Game Procedure#^tr-406-1-h|Start of Game Procedure]]. does not apply to “best of 1” limited competitions. ^tr-602-3-g

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.g · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.g · PDF page 27</div></details>

For [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]] generation and [[Deck Construction|deck construction]] rules specific to each limited format, see Limited Formats below. ^tr-602-3-h

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.3.h · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.3.h · PDF page 27</div></details>

### Limited Formats

<span title="Tournament rule 602.4"></span> ^tr-602-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4 · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4 · PDF page 27</div></details>

### Sealed

<span title="Tournament rule 602.4.a"></span> ^tr-602-4-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a · PDF page 27</div></details>

[[Sealed]] is a limited format where players are each provided with 6 unopened Riftbound booster packs to open and use to construct their deck. ^tr-602-4-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.1 · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.1 · PDF page 27</div></details>

Main Decks for [[Sealed|sealed]] decks must be at least 25 [[Card|cards]], but can be more. ^tr-602-4-a-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.2 · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.2 · PDF page 27</div></details>

The [[Domain Identity|domain identity]] of a [[Sealed|sealed]] deck is any three domains, or any [[Domain|domain]] plus the domains of the deck’s [[Champion Legend]]. ^tr-602-4-a-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.3 · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.3 · PDF page 27</div></details>

A [[Sealed|sealed]] deck may have any [[Champion|Champion Unit]] that is included in its [[Domain Identity|domain identity]] as its [[Chosen Champion]], even one that does not have a [[Champion Tag|champion tag]] that matches its [[Champion Legend]] (if it has one). ^tr-602-4-a-3-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.3.a · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.3.a · PDF page 27</div></details>

A [[Sealed|sealed]] deck may contain any [[Signature|signature]] [[Spell|spells]] that are fully included in its [[Domain Identity|domain identity]], even if the deck does not include the corresponding [[Champion|champion]]. ^tr-602-4-a-3-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.3.b · PDF page 27" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.3.b · PDF page 27</div></details>

Example: A [[Sealed|sealed]] deck with a [[Domain Identity|domain identity]] of Fury/Body/Order could use [[Signature|signature]] [[Spell|spells]] that are Fury/Order or Body/Order, but not signature spells that are Fury/Mind or Body/Chaos. ^tr-602-4-a-3-b-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.3.b.1 · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.3.b.1 · PDF page 28</div></details>

Players may only include [[Rune|Runes]] from any source in their [[Rune Deck]] that match the [[Domain Identity|domain identity]] of their [[Sealed|sealed]] deck. ^tr-602-4-a-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.4 · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.4 · PDF page 28</div></details>

A [[Sealed|sealed]] deck is still legal if it has no [[Champion Legend]] or [[Chosen Champion]]. ^tr-602-4-a-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.5 · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.5 · PDF page 28</div></details>

A player may opt not to use a [[Champion Legend]] or a [[Chosen Champion]] in their [[Sealed|sealed]] deck even if they have one in their provided [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]]. ^tr-602-4-a-5-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.5.a · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.5.a · PDF page 28</div></details>

If a player's deck is missing a [[Champion Legend]] and/or a [[Chosen Champion]], they [[Draw|draw]] a [[Card|card]] at the start of their first [[Beginning Phase]] each game. (If both are missing, they still only draw one card.) ^tr-602-4-a-5-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.5.b · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.5.b · PDF page 28</div></details>

The normal restrictions on quantities of named or [[Signature|signature cards]] do not apply. For example, a player may use 10 Pouty Poro in their [[Main Deck]] if they received them in the provided [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]]. ^tr-602-4-a-6

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.6 · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.6 · PDF page 28</div></details>

The “[[Unique]]” [[Keywords|keyword]] does not apply in [[Sealed|sealed]]. ^tr-602-4-a-6-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.6.a · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.6.a · PDF page 28</div></details>

A [[Sealed|sealed]] deck’s [[Sideboard|sideboard]] is every [[Card|card]] provided to the player as part of the competition that isn’t used in their starting deck configuration, unless otherwise specified. See [[Sideboard#^tr-403|Sideboard]] for more information on sideboards. ^tr-602-4-a-7

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.7 · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.7 · PDF page 28</div></details>

Additionally, players may change their [[Domain Identity|domain identity]], [[Rune Deck]], [[Legend]], and [[Chosen Champion]] while [[Sideboard|sideboarding]] as long as the resulting deck still meets [[Limited#^tr-602-4-a-2|Limited]]-[[Limited#^tr-602-4-a-4|Limited]]. ^tr-602-4-a-7-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.7.a · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.7.a · PDF page 28</div></details>

### Sealed Pool Registration & Deck Construction

<span title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8"></span> ^tr-602-4-a-8

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8 · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8 · PDF page 28</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], the [[Head Judge|head judge]] must require registration of the [[Sealed|sealed]] pool prior to [[Deck Construction|deck construction]]. ^tr-602-4-a-8-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.a · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.a · PDF page 28</div></details>

Each player receives the unopened product at their seat. ^tr-602-4-a-8-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.b · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.b · PDF page 28</div></details>

Players on one side of the table (A) open their product, observed by players on the other side (B). ^tr-602-4-a-8-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.c · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.c · PDF page 28</div></details>

Players verify that the product has the correct undamaged quantity of [[Card|cards]] and rarity distribution. If a pack with an error is found, call a judge to replace the pack if possible. ^tr-602-4-a-8-d

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.d · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.d · PDF page 28</div></details>

Once all unopened product has been opened and observed by B, those [[Card|cards]] are placed next to B. ^tr-602-4-a-8-e

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.e · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.e · PDF page 28</div></details>

[[Repeat]] the steps outlined in [[Limited#^tr-602-4-a-8-c|the opening and pool-handoff steps]] but with (B) opening and (A) observing. ^tr-602-4-a-8-f

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.f · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.f · PDF page 28</div></details>

Each player now sorts and registers the contents of the pool placed next to them. ^tr-602-4-a-8-g

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.g · PDF page 28" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.g · PDF page 28</div></details>

After registration is complete, each player returns the pool and registration sheet to the other player. ^tr-602-4-a-8-h

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.h · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.h · PDF page 29</div></details>

Each player verifies that their own pool has been registered correctly. ^tr-602-4-a-8-i

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.i · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.i · PDF page 29</div></details>

[[Deck Construction|Deck construction]] begins. ^tr-602-4-a-8-j

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.j · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.j · PDF page 29</div></details>

It’s recommended that players be given 20 minutes for [[Deck Registration|deck registration]] and 30 minutes for [[Deck Construction|deck construction]]. Deck construction can be increased to 45 minutes for [[Casual OPL|low OPL]] events where players are experiencing the [[Card|cards]] for the first time, such as pre-rift and release events. ^tr-602-4-a-8-k

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.k · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.k · PDF page 29</div></details>

The starting deck configuration and [[Sideboard|sideboard]] are registered on the same sheet as the total [[Limited Card Pool|card pool]]. ^tr-602-4-a-8-l

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.l · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.8.l · PDF page 29</div></details>

### Preconstructed Decks in Sealed Competitions

<span title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.9"></span> ^tr-602-4-a-9

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.9 · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.9 · PDF page 29</div></details>

Some competitions or portions of competitions provide preconstructed decks for players to use. These are considered [[Sealed|sealed]] decks. ^tr-602-4-a-9-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.9.a · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.9.a · PDF page 29</div></details>

Players must use exactly the decks provided, except as modified by any addenda to the competition. ^tr-602-4-a-9-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.9.b · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.9.b · PDF page 29</div></details>

This may override the [[Sealed|sealed]] [[Deck Construction|deck construction]] rules above. ^tr-602-4-a-9-b-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.a.9.b.1 · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.a.9.b.1 · PDF page 29</div></details>

### Draft

<span title="Tournament rule 602.4.b"></span> ^tr-602-4-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b · PDF page 29</div></details>

[[Draft]] is a limited format where players are each provided with 3 unopened Riftbound booster packs to open and draft amongst other players. ^tr-602-4-b-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.1 · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.1 · PDF page 29</div></details>

[[Draft]] groups consist of as close to eight players as possible, seated randomly around a table. Draft process is as follows: ^tr-602-4-b-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2 · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2 · PDF page 29</div></details>

Each player opens one of their boosters, removes the [[Rune]] or [[Token|token]], then looks through the remaining contents to ensure there are thirteen undamaged [[Card|cards]] in the pack with a correct rarity distribution. ^tr-602-4-b-2-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.a · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.a · PDF page 29</div></details>

Players can keep the [[Rune]] or [[Token|token]] they opened. ^tr-602-4-b-2-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.a.1 · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.a.1 · PDF page 29</div></details>

If an opened pack has an error, players must call a judge to replace the pack if possible. ^tr-602-4-b-2-a-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.a.2 · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.a.2 · PDF page 29</div></details>

Once pack contents are verified, players will look through the [[Card|cards]] in their pack and select one to [[Draft|draft]] to the top of a single unobscured facedown pile in front of them, then pass the remaining contents facedown to the player to their left. Players [[Repeat|repeat]] this process with the cards passed to them until there are no cards remaining. ^tr-602-4-b-2-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b · PDF page 29</div></details>

Once the first pack is completed, [[Repeat|repeat]] [[Limited#^tr-602-4-b-2-a|Limited]]-[[Limited#^tr-602-4-b-2-b|Limited]] with the second pack, except passing [[Card|cards]] to the right. ^tr-602-4-b-2-b-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b.1 · PDF page 29" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b.1 · PDF page 29</div></details>

Once the second pack is completed, [[Repeat|repeat]] [[Limited#^tr-602-4-b-2-a|Limited]]-[[Limited#^tr-602-4-b-2-b|Limited]] with the third pack, again passing [[Card|cards]] to the left. ^tr-602-4-b-2-b-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b.2 · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b.2 · PDF page 30</div></details>

Once a [[Card|card]] has been added to a player’s single facedown pile in front of them it is considered selected and can’t be returned to the pack. ^tr-602-4-b-2-b-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b.3 · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b.3 · PDF page 30</div></details>

Players must make a reasonable effort to conceal the front face of [[Card|cards]] they have selected and cards in their current pack from other players. ^tr-602-4-b-2-b-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b.4 · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.b.4 · PDF page 30</div></details>

Once this process is complete, each player should have 39 [[Card|cards]] to use as their [[Limited Card Pool|limited card pool]]. ^tr-602-4-b-2-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.c · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.c · PDF page 30</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], players may not use [[Electronic Devices|electronic devices]] to take notes or as a strategic aid during drafting. At [[Casual OPL|low OPL]], this can be allowed by the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. ^tr-602-4-b-2-d

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.d · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.d · PDF page 30</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], players may not receive any [[Outside Assistance|outside assistance]] during drafting. At [[Casual OPL|low OPL]], this can be allowed by the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. ^tr-602-4-b-2-e

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.e · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.e · PDF page 30</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], during the [[Draft|draft]] process players may not [[Reveal|reveal]] [[Card|cards]] they have seen or selected, or openly discuss the draft. At [[Casual OPL|low OPL]], this can be allowed by the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. ^tr-602-4-b-2-f

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.f · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.f · PDF page 30</div></details>

During the [[Draft|draft]] process, players are not permitted to seek knowledge of [[Card|cards]] fronts other drafters are viewing or have already selected through any means. Attempting to do so is [[Cheating|cheating]]. ^tr-602-4-b-2-g

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.g · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.g · PDF page 30</div></details>

It is not [[Cheating|cheating]] for a player to see [[Card|card]] backs of cards other players are viewing or of cards players have added to the facedown pile in front of them. ^tr-602-4-b-2-g-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.g.1 · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.g.1 · PDF page 30</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], players can’t look at the [[Card|cards]] they have already drafted, except during intervals designated by a judge between packs. At [[Casual OPL|low OPL]], this is allowed at any time as long as the player is not [[Hold|holding]] other cards at the same time. ^tr-602-4-b-2-h

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.h · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.h · PDF page 30</div></details>

At [[Professional OPL|professional OPL]], drafts must be timed. At [[Competitive OPL|competitive OPL]], drafts are timed at the discretion of the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. At [[Casual OPL|casual OPL]], drafts are untimed but players must [[Draft|draft]] at a reasonable pace. ^tr-602-4-b-2-i

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.i · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.2.i · PDF page 30</div></details>

Main Decks for [[Draft|draft]] decks must be at least 20 [[Card|cards]], but can be more. ^tr-602-4-b-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.3 · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.3 · PDF page 30</div></details>

The [[Domain Identity|domain identity]] of a [[Draft|draft]] deck is any three domains, or any [[Domain|domain]] plus the domains of the deck’s [[Champion Legend]]. ^tr-602-4-b-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.4 · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.4 · PDF page 30</div></details>

A [[Draft|draft]] deck may have any [[Champion|Champion Unit]] that is included in its [[Domain Identity|domain identity]] as its [[Chosen Champion]], even one that does not have a [[Champion Tag|champion tag]] that matches its [[Champion Legend]] (if it has one). ^tr-602-4-b-4-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.4.a · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.4.a · PDF page 30</div></details>

A [[Draft|draft]] deck may contain any [[Signature|signature]] [[Spell|spells]] that are fully included in its [[Domain Identity|domain identity]], even if the deck does not include the corresponding [[Champion|champion]]. ^tr-602-4-b-4-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.4.b · PDF page 30" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.4.b · PDF page 30</div></details>

Example: A [[Draft|draft]] deck with a [[Domain Identity|domain identity]] of Fury/Body/Order could use [[Signature|signature]] [[Spell|spells]] that are Fury/Order or Body/Order, but not signature spells that are Fury/Mind or Body/Chaos. ^tr-602-4-b-4-b-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.4.b.1 · PDF page 31" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.4.b.1 · PDF page 31</div></details>

Players may only include [[Rune|Runes]] from any source in their [[Rune Deck]] that match the [[Domain Identity|domain identity]] of their [[Draft|draft]] deck. ^tr-602-4-b-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.5 · PDF page 31" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.5 · PDF page 31</div></details>

A [[Draft|draft]] deck is still legal if it has no [[Champion Legend]] or [[Chosen Champion]]. ^tr-602-4-b-6

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.6 · PDF page 31" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.6 · PDF page 31</div></details>

A player may opt not to use a [[Champion Legend]] or a [[Chosen Champion]] in their [[Draft|draft]] deck even if they have them among their drafted [[Card|cards]]. ^tr-602-4-b-6-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.6.a · PDF page 31" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.6.a · PDF page 31</div></details>

If a player's deck is missing a [[Champion Legend]] and/or a [[Chosen Champion]], they [[Draw|draw]] a [[Card|card]] at the start of their first [[Beginning Phase]] each game. (If both are missing, they still only draw one card.) ^tr-602-4-b-6-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.6.b · PDF page 31" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.6.b · PDF page 31</div></details>

The normal restrictions on quantities of named or [[Signature|signature cards]] do not apply. For example, a player may use 10 Pouty Poro in their [[Main Deck]] if they selected them during the [[Draft|draft]]. ^tr-602-4-b-7

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.7 · PDF page 31" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.7 · PDF page 31</div></details>

The “[[Unique]]” [[Keywords|keyword]] does not apply in [[Draft|draft]]. ^tr-602-4-b-7-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.7.a · PDF page 31" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.7.a · PDF page 31</div></details>

A [[Draft|draft]] deck’s [[Sideboard|sideboard]] is every [[Card|card]] drafted by the player as part of the competition that isn’t used in their starting deck configuration, unless otherwise specified. See [[Sideboard#^tr-403|Sideboard]] for more information on sideboards. ^tr-602-4-b-8

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.8 · PDF page 31" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.8 · PDF page 31</div></details>

Additionally, players may change their [[Domain Identity|domain identity]], [[Rune Deck]], [[Legend]], and [[Chosen Champion]] while [[Sideboard|sideboarding]] as long as the resulting deck still meets [[Limited#^tr-602-4-b-3|Limited]]-[[Limited#^tr-602-4-b-5|Limited]]. ^tr-602-4-b-8-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 602.4.b.8.a · PDF page 31" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 602.4.b.8.a · PDF page 31</div></details>
