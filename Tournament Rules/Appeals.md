# Appeals

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Tournament Rules|Tournament rules]]

<span title="Tournament rule 413"></span> ^tr-413

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 413 · PDF page 13" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 413 · PDF page 13</div></details>

If a player disagrees with a judge’s ruling, they may appeal to the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. ^tr-413-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 413.1 · PDF page 13" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 413.1 · PDF page 13</div></details>

At [[High OPL|high OPL]], the [[Head Judge|head judge]] may designate certain [[Floor Judge|floor judges]] to be appeals judges, who are then empowered to hear and rule on appeals. In such cases, those judges must wear uniforms matching the head judge’s uniform. ^tr-413-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 413.2 · PDF page 13" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 413.2 · PDF page 13</div></details>

Players may not appeal before a full ruling is made by a judge. ^tr-413-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 413.3 · PDF page 13" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 413.3 · PDF page 13</div></details>

Players may not appeal a ruling of an appeals judge or the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. ^tr-413-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 413.4 · PDF page 13" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 413.4 · PDF page 13</div></details>

If a [[Penalties|penalty]] is applied outside the scope of a competition, the appeals process should follow what’s laid out on the official Riot Games website. &#91;citation needed&#93; ^tr-413-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 413.5 · PDF page 13" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 413.5 · PDF page 13</div></details>
