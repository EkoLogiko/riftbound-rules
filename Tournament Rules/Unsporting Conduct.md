### Overview

<span title="Tournament rule 704.1"></span> ^tr-704-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.1 · PDF page 47" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.1 · PDF page 47</div></details>

Riftbound does not tolerate unsporting conduct. Tournament attendees and [[Spectator|spectators]] must behave in a respectful and honorable manner. ^tr-704-1-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.1.a · PDF page 47" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.1.a · PDF page 47</div></details>

If a player commits the same [[Card Category|category]] of unsporting conduct &#91;[[Warning]]&#93; 2 or more times in the same event, the [[Penalties|penalty]] is upgraded to &#91;[[Game Loss]]&#93; and may be further upgraded at the discretion of the [[Head Judge|head judge]]. ^tr-704-1-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.1.b · PDF page 47" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.1.b · PDF page 47</div></details>

Infractions and [[Penalties|penalties]] listed in this section aim to serve as general guidelines. Listed infractions are examples and are not exhaustive. All judges are empowered to escalate unsporting conduct issues and the [[Head Judge|head judge]] may apply any penalty they deem appropriate for unsporting conduct, up to and including &#91;[[Disqualification]]&#93;. ^tr-704-1-c

[[Bribery]] &#91;[[Match Loss]]&#93;: Tournament attendees and [[Spectator|spectators]] may not bribe tournament players or tournament officials. ^tr-704-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.5 · PDF page 48" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.5 · PDF page 48</div></details>

The outcome of tournament games may not be influenced by offers of out-of-game rewards or incentives. ^tr-704-5-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.5.a · PDF page 48" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.5.a · PDF page 48</div></details>

Making or accepting such an offer is considered [[Bribery|bribery]]. ^tr-704-5-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.5.a.1 · PDF page 48" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.5.a.1 · PDF page 48</div></details>

[[Bribery]] applies to actions within a game, not just the final results. ^tr-704-5-a-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.5.a.2 · PDF page 48" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.5.a.2 · PDF page 48</div></details>

Receiving an offer is not considered [[Bribery|bribery]] unless entertained or accepted, but the receiving player must inform a judge. ^tr-704-5-a-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.5.a.3 · PDF page 48" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.5.a.3 · PDF page 48</div></details>

Sharing prizes that have not yet been awarded in the current tournament does not constitute [[Bribery|bribery]]. Players are permitted to make such agreements before or during their match, provided that the prize sharing is not offered or accepted in return for the outcome of a game or match, any in-game action, or a player [[Dropping|dropping]] from the tournament. ^tr-704-5-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.5.b · PDF page 48" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.5.b · PDF page 48</div></details>

It is not [[Bribery|bribery]] when players in the single-elimination portion of a tournament offering only items such as cash, store credit, and/or unopened product, with the [[Competition Organizer|competition organizer]]’s permission, agree to divide the subsequent tournament prizes evenly. All players still in the tournament must agree to the arrangement. ^tr-704-5-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.5.c · PDF page 48" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.5.c · PDF page 48</div></details>

Players may end their participation in the tournament at this point or continue to [[Play|play]]. If this is a major streamed event, players must continue to play for final rankings. ^tr-704-5-c-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.5.c.1 · PDF page 48" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.5.c.1 · PDF page 48</div></details>

It is not [[Bribery|bribery]] when, in the final round of the single-elimination portion of a tournament, with the [[Competition Organizer|competition organizer]]’s permission, players mutually agree on a winner and how to divide the remaining prizes. In such cases, one player must agree to drop from the tournament. Prizes are then awarded based on each player’s final standing. ^tr-704-5-d

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.5.d · PDF page 48-49" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.5.d · PDF page 48-49</div></details>

[[Wagering]] &#91;[[Disqualification]]&#93;: Tournament attendees may not wager on any portion of a tournament, match, or game, including its outcome. ^tr-704-6

[[Theft]] &#91;[[Disqualification]]&#93;: Stealing or attempting to steal the property of a tournament attendee, [[Spectator|spectator]], vendor, or event organizer. ^tr-704-7

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.7 · PDF page 49" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.7 · PDF page 49</div></details>

[[Cheating]] &#91;[[Disqualification]]&#93;: Riftbound does not tolerate cheating at any [[Level|level]] of [[Play|play]]. ^tr-704-8

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.8 · PDF page 49" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.8 · PDF page 49</div></details>

[[Cheating]] includes but is not [[Limited|limited]] to performing any action that results in a [[Penalties|penalty]] listed under 702 or 703 if it is done knowingly or maliciously to obtain an advantage. ^tr-704-8-a

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.8.a · PDF page 49" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.8.a · PDF page 49</div></details>

This includes attempting to create game errors to use a remedy to the player’s  advantage. ^tr-704-8-a-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.8.a.1 · PDF page 49" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.8.a.1 · PDF page 49</div></details>

If judges suspect a player is violating the rules knowingly or maliciously, the [[Head Judge|head judge]] may initiate an investigation to determine if [[Cheating|cheating]] occurred. ^tr-704-8-a-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.8.a.2 · PDF page 49" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.8.a.2 · PDF page 49</div></details>

Lying to a tournament official is [[Cheating|cheating]]. ^tr-704-8-b

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.8.b · PDF page 49" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.8.b · PDF page 49</div></details>

Intentionally neglecting to indicate a teammate's error is [[Cheating|cheating]]. ^tr-704-8-c

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 704.8.c · PDF page 49" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 704.8.c · PDF page 49</div></details>
