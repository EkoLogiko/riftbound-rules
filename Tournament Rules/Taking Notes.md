# Taking Notes

[[Start Here|Home]] · [[Concept Index|Find a concept]] · [[Tournament Rules|Tournament rules]]

<span title="Tournament rule 416"></span> ^tr-416

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 416 · PDF page 14" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 416 · PDF page 14</div></details>

Players may take written notes during a match and refer to those notes during games in the same match. ^tr-416-1

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 416.1 · PDF page 14" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 416.1 · PDF page 14</div></details>

At the beginning of each match, each player’s written note sheet must be empty and visible to all opponents and tournament officials. ^tr-416-2

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 416.2 · PDF page 14" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 416.2 · PDF page 14</div></details>

Players do not have to [[Reveal|reveal]] their written notes to opponents. Judges may ask players to reveal and/or explain their notes. ^tr-416-3

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 416.3 · PDF page 14" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 416.3 · PDF page 14</div></details>

Players may not refer to notes from outside the current match, including written notes from previous matches, during games. ^tr-416-4

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 416.4 · PDF page 14" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 416.4 · PDF page 14</div></details>

Between games, players may refer to written notes they made prior to the match. They do not have to [[Reveal|reveal]] these notes to opponents. Players may consult such notes during [[Sideboard|sideboarding]] and after [[Battlefield|battlefields]] are revealed, but must stop once opening hands are drawn (before mulligans). ^tr-416-5

<details class="rule-ref"><summary title="Tournament rule 416.5 · PDF page 14" aria-label="Show source rule">ⓘ</summary><div class="rule-tooltip">Tournament rule 416.5 · PDF page 14</div></details>
