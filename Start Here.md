## Reading on your phone

Use **Reading view** for the cleanest layout. Search for a concept, open its note, and follow the links. The ⓘ icon beside a passage holds its original rule number and PDF page: hover on desktop, or tap to open it on a phone. Tap again to close it.

The vault includes an enabled CSS snippet for compact reference icons and desktop hover tooltips. Without the snippet, the icons still expand to show the reference. Rule numbers and markup are visible in Source mode because they are stored in the Markdown.

## Open the vault

On desktop, choose **Open folder as vault** and select this folder. On your phone, transfer the whole folder, including `.obsidian`, using your usual vault sync or file transfer method, then open it as a vault. If the styling is absent, enable **riftbound-rules** in Settings → Appearance → CSS snippets.
